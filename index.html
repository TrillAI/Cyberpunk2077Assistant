03 // ARSENAL

High-end guns and exactly where they live

Iconic weapons are unique, one-per-save guns with a fixed special effect. On the PS4 build they are also missable: if a quest ends and you didn't loot the body, that gun is gone for that playthrough. Each card below tells you the district, the quest, the exact route, and the moment it becomes permanent.

⚠ The rule of three for missable iconics

  1. Loot the body before you leave the area. Some bodies despawn, some quest areas lock.
  2. Don't sell iconics. Even a gun you hate is display-wall material and, more importantly, upgradeable. Selling one is a one-way trip.
  3. Don't scrap the crafting spec. If a gun comes from a crafting spec, that spec is the only way to make a higher-tier copy later.
